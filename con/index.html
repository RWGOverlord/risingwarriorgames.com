Shipping rates

Shipping is charged separately from the item price and is worked out from the weight of the box, so it depends on your region and how many playmats are in the order. The exact amount appears in the order panel above before you pay. Local pickup in Chattanooga is free.

Rates by region are shown in the order panel above once you pick an edition, or email overlord@risingwarriorgames.com and we will quote it.

  • Local pickup is free. If you are in the Chattanooga area you can collect the box in person instead of paying for shipping. The date, time and place are in the order panel above.
  • The Misfits Mini Expansion and the Life Point Trackers are light and ship in the same package. They do not change your shipping cost. Playmats do, because they need a larger box.
