The studio

Games where you take command

Rising Warrior Games is an independent studio focused on competitive, system-driven tabletop experiences where players take control instead of just reacting.

Our games are built around meaningful decisions, aggressive interaction, and mechanics that reward strategy over luck. Every release builds on the same foundation: strong mechanics, a distinct identity, and systems that reward players who think ahead.

We make games that put players in command, where strategy, chaos and personality collide.
