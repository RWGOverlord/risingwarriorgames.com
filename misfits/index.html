Misfits Mini Expansion

Survivor Replacement Guide

A new cast. The same balanced game.

The Misfits Mini Expansion brings 20 new characters to Zombie Overlord. Swap in all 20, play with one collection, or mix and match your favorites. Just be sure to remove the matching base-game cards when you add expansion characters.

Swap all 20

Want to use the entire expansion?

  1. Find the base-game Survivor cards marked with the Swap Icon, a small white circle with a rune inside, in the top right corner.
  2. Remove all 20 of those marked cards.
  3. Add all 20 character cards from the Misfits Mini Expansion.

You're ready to play!

Mix and match

Only want some of the Misfits?

No problem. You can use an entire collection or just the individual characters you like. For each expansion card you add, remove the exact base-game card paired with it below. That keeps your deck the same size.

For the intended gameplay experience, swap all 20. Partial swaps are allowed, but they may affect game balance.

Replace, don't add!

Misfits cards are replacements, not extra cards to shuffle into the base game. For every expansion card you include, remove its matching base-game card using the guide below.
