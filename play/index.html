You are not surviving the apocalypse. You are running it.

Zombie Overlord is a competitive deck-builder where you play the villain. Set traps to lure desperate survivors into your base, convert them into your own horde, and tear down the other Overlords before they do the same to you.

Every survivor you take is one your rivals cannot have, so the board is a fight from the first turn. No waiting politely while everyone builds an engine.
