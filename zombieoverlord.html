About the game

The Dead Rise at Your Command

Most zombie games make you run. Zombie Overlord lets you hunt.

You don't save survivors — you harvest them. Set traps. Lure the desperate into your base. Then flip them into zombies and add them to your growing horde.

Every turn, your deck gets stronger. Every rival gets closer to being overrun. Outplay your opponents, sabotage their plans, and prove there's only room for one ruler of the apocalypse.

What makes it unique

Three things that set it apart

Zombie Overlord isn't just another zombie game. It's a strategic engine built around deception, conversion and chaos.

Trap-and-convert gameplay

You don't just lure survivors — you turn them into your army.

Survivors roam the wasteland in desperation. By placing traps that match the resources they're searching for, you lure them to your base and convert them into loyal undead minions.

Every survivor you claim permanently strengthens your deck, unlocking stronger attacks, better combos and new strategic paths. But every survivor is contested — attack your rivals, sabotage their traps, or out-lure them entirely.

Strategic trap warfare

Your traps are both your weapons — and your greatest vulnerability.

Trap cards shape the battlefield and define your strategy, each tied to different resource types that fuel combos, attacks and disruption. Build deadly combos, bait opponents into bad decisions, or watch your plans crumble when rivals strike.

  • Sabotage enemy traps to steal their advantage
  • Strike directly or dismantle their trap network
  • No plan survives contact with rival Overlords
  • Every turn reshapes the battlefield

50+ unique characters

Every game feels different.

Each character features original, hand-crafted artwork that brings the wasteland to life — from desperate civilians to dangerous criminals. Each one feels like a character, not just a card.

And when you convert them, they become yours forever.

Every character, every background and most of the visual identity of this game is the work of Maximiliano Musso — Chino the Goat. He has stuck with the project for years and been endlessly patient with me. That's his own self-portrait, zombified.

How to play

A turn in five steps

Each round is fast, aggressive and full of decisions. Here's how a turn unfolds.

Gain resources & buy cards

Play Supply cards to build up Purchase Power, then spend it on new Traps, Action cards or Scavengers from the shared Wasteland.

Build your base & activate abilities

Place Trap cards into your base to set up defenses and lure types. Activate their special abilities for an edge — more resources, card draws or disruption.

Command your horde

Play Minion cards to attack rival bases. Hit their Life Points directly, or break and deactivate their Traps to disrupt their lure strategy before they can use it against you.

Lure & convert survivors

Survivors in the Wasteland are drawn to whoever has the most matching resources across their active Traps. If you lure them, they flip into Zombie Minions and join your deck permanently.

Clean up & draw new cards

Discard unused cards and draw back up. Your newly converted zombies shuffle into your deck, making every turn stronger than the last.

Become the Overlord

Last player standing with Life Points wins. Out-lure, out-build and out-battle your rivals to claim your rotten throne.

Before you order

Common questions

Anything not covered here, email hello@risingwarriorgames.com and you'll get a real answer.

How many players is it, and how long does a game take?

Two to four players, 30–40 minutes per game, recommended for ages 14 and up. The box holds 194 cards and more than 50 unique characters.

Is there a solo mode?

No. Zombie Overlord is a competitive game built for 2–4 players — luring survivors away from rivals and attacking their bases is the core of it, so it needs at least one opponent.

Can I read the rules before I buy?

Yes. The full rulebook is available to read (PDF, 6 MB) before you order. If you'd rather watch than read, there's a complete playthrough on this page — about eight minutes from setup to the point where the conversion engine takes over.

What's the difference between the three editions?

All three include the complete 194-card core game and every stretch goal unlocked during the campaign.

  • Core Edition ($34) — the game on its own.
  • Premium Bundle ($46) — adds the Misfits Mini Expansion.
  • Battlefield Edition ($88) — adds the Misfits Mini Expansion and a double playmat set in any two designs.
Do I need the Misfits Mini Expansion to play?

No. The Core Edition is the complete game. The Misfits Mini Expansion is optional — it adds 20 more characters, including the Holiday Horde and backer-created cards, and is fully compatible with the core game.

How much is shipping?

From $8–$12 in the US, $18–$22 to Canada and $22–$28 to the rest of the world for the game on its own. Full rates by region and order size are listed above. Small add-ons like the dice, mini expansion and holographic cards ship in the same package and don't raise your shipping cost.

When will my order arrive?

The print run leaves the factory in China this week and fulfillment begins in November 2026. Shipping is charged separately from the item price and confirmed through BackerKit based on your address and what's in your order.

Can I cancel or return my order?
  • Before it ships — cancel any time for a full refund. Email hello@risingwarriorgames.com.
  • After it arrives — unopened and still in shrinkwrap, within 30 days of delivery. Return shipping is on you; we refund the full purchase price once it reaches us.
  • Damaged or missing cards — we replace them free, no return needed and no deadline. Send a photo and we'll post replacements out to you.
  • EU and UK customers have 14 days from delivery to cancel for any reason, as required by law.
Where does checkout happen?

Through BackerKit, which handles the preorder store and payment. Rising Warrior Games is an independent studio in Chattanooga, Tennessee, and fulfills orders directly with regional shipping partners.

Will it be available in stores?

Not right now — the BackerKit store is the only place to get it. We'd like to see Zombie Overlord on the shelf at local game shops and with a handful of online retailers eventually, but nothing is arranged yet and there's no date to share.

One thing to know either way: the add-ons are campaign unlocks and stay exclusive to the BackerKit store. The Misfits Mini Expansion, the playmats and the engraved life trackers aren't part of any future shop edition.
