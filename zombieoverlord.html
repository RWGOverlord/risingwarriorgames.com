About the game

The Dead Rise at Your Command

Most zombie games make you run. Zombie Overlord lets you hunt.

You don't save survivors — you harvest them. Set traps. Lure the desperate into your base. Then flip them into zombies and add them to your growing horde.

Every turn, your deck gets stronger. Every rival gets closer to being overrun. Outplay your opponents, sabotage their plans, and prove there's only room for one ruler of the apocalypse.

What makes it unique

Three things that set it apart

Zombie Overlord isn't just another zombie game. It's a strategic engine built around deception, conversion and chaos.

Trap-and-convert gameplay

You don't just lure survivors — you turn them into your army.

Survivors roam the wasteland in desperation. By placing traps that match the resources they're searching for, you lure them to your base and convert them into loyal undead minions.

Every survivor you claim permanently strengthens your deck, unlocking stronger attacks, better combos and new strategic paths. But every survivor is contested — attack your rivals, sabotage their traps, or out-lure them entirely.

Strategic trap warfare

Your traps are both your weapons — and your greatest vulnerability.

Trap cards shape the battlefield and define your strategy, each tied to different resource types that fuel combos, attacks and disruption. Build deadly combos, bait opponents into bad decisions, or watch your plans crumble when rivals strike.

  • Sabotage enemy traps to steal their advantage
  • Strike directly or dismantle their trap network
  • No plan survives contact with rival Overlords
  • Every turn reshapes the battlefield

How to play

A turn in five steps

Each round is fast, aggressive and full of decisions. Here's how a turn unfolds.

Gain resources & buy cards

Play Supply cards to build up Purchase Power, then spend it on new Traps, Action cards or Scavengers from the shared Wasteland.

Build your base & activate abilities

Place Trap cards into your base to set up defenses and lure types. Activate their special abilities for an edge — more resources, card draws or disruption.

Command your horde

Play Minion cards to attack rival bases. Hit their Life Points directly, or break and deactivate their Traps to disrupt their lure strategy before they can use it against you.

Lure & convert survivors

Survivors in the Wasteland are drawn to whoever has the most matching resources across their active Traps. If you lure them, they flip into Zombie Minions and join your deck permanently.

Clean up & draw new cards

Discard unused cards and draw back up. Your newly converted zombies shuffle into your deck, making every turn stronger than the last.

Become the Overlord

Last player standing with Life Points wins. Out-lure, out-build and out-battle your rivals to claim your rotten throne.
